# QuickBite Delivery Zones and Timing

## Zone A — Central Cairo (Maadi, Zamalek, Downtown)
- Average delivery time: 20-30 minutes
- Delivery fee: 15 EGP
- Operating hours: 8:00 AM - 2:00 AM
- Surge pricing applies during peak hours (12:00-2:00 PM, 7:00-10:00 PM): +5 EGP

## Zone B — Greater Cairo (Heliopolis, Nasr City, 6th of October)
- Average delivery time: 35-50 minutes
- Delivery fee: 25 EGP
- Operating hours: 9:00 AM - 12:00 AM
- Minimum order value: 100 EGP

## Zone C — Suburban Areas (New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed, 10th of Ramadan)
- Average delivery time: 45-70 minutes
- Delivery fee: 40 EGP
- Operating hours: 10:00 AM - 11:00 PM
- Minimum order value: 150 EGP

## Special Conditions
- During Ramadan, delivery hours shift to 3:00 PM - 4:00 AM across all zones.
- Heavy rain or sandstorm conditions may temporarily suspend deliveries with full order cancellation available.
- Friday prayer time (12:00-2:00 PM) may experience extended delivery times in all zones.
