# QuickBite Refund Policy

## Overview
QuickBite offers refunds for orders that do not meet our quality standards or delivery guarantees.

## Eligibility
- Orders delivered more than 30 minutes past the estimated delivery time are eligible for a full refund.
- Orders with missing items qualify for a partial refund equal to the cost of the missing items plus a 10% inconvenience credit.
- Orders with incorrect items qualify for a full replacement or full refund at the customer's choice.

## Process
1. Customers must submit a refund request within 24 hours of delivery.
2. Requests are reviewed within 2 business days.
3. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 3-5 business days.

## Exceptions
- Orders cancelled by the customer after the restaurant has begun preparation are subject to a 20% cancellation fee.
- Refunds are not available for orders where the customer provided an incorrect delivery address.
- Promotional or discounted orders are refunded at the discounted price, not the original price.
