# QuickBite Partner Restaurant Program

## Joining Requirements
- Valid commercial food license from the Egyptian Food Safety Authority
- Minimum food safety rating of 3/5 stars
- Ability to prepare orders within 15 minutes of receiving them
- Kitchen operating at least 6 days per week

## Commission Structure
- Standard commission: 18% per order
- Premium partners (rating 4.5+): 15% commission
- New partners: 12% introductory rate for the first 3 months
- Exclusive partners (QuickBite only): 10% commission

## Technology Requirements
- Partners must use the QuickBite Partner Tablet App for order management
- Real-time menu updates must be maintained (prices, availability)
- Average order acceptance time must be under 2 minutes
- Partners who consistently exceed 5-minute acceptance time may face temporary delisting

## Quality Standards
- Customer rating must maintain a minimum of 3.8/5.0 over a rolling 30-day period
- Food hygiene inspections are conducted quarterly by QuickBite's quality team
- Three consecutive ratings below 3.5 trigger a mandatory improvement review
- Partners with ratings below 3.0 for 60 consecutive days are removed from the platform

## Payment Terms
- Weekly settlements every Monday for the previous week's orders
- Payment via bank transfer to the registered business account
- Disputes must be raised within 14 days of settlement
